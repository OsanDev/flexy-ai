# FLEXY AI v4.2.67: Animated Caption Engine

## What's new
- Word reveal animation progressively reveals caption words during the caption's opening animation window.
- Karaoke highlight advances across words evenly over the caption's timeline duration.
- Pop, fade, and slide-up entrance animations.
- Per-caption animation selection and adjustable animation time (0.1 to 2.0 seconds).
- Existing caption typography, color, background, placement, and preset controls remain available.

## Timing note
Karaoke highlighting is evenly distributed across the caption duration. It is a visual preview effect, not phoneme- or word-timestamp synchronization. Word-level speech synchronization requires word timestamps from a transcription source.
