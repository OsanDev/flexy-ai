# FLEXY AI v4.2.76: Track visibility and audio solo

- Video and audio track visibility controls exclude hidden tracks from the generated render graph without deleting their clips from the project.
- Caption track visibility excludes captions from the generated render graph while retaining caption edits in the project.
- Audio solo selects one audio track for export. Other audio tracks remain in the project but are rendered at zero volume. Toggle solo again to restore the normal mix.
- Visibility and solo are timeline-document settings, so they follow the existing project persistence and undo/redo flow.
- Existing mute and track lock controls remain available independently.
